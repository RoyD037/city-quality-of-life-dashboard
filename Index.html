Methodology & Definitions
  • Positive includes Excellent and Good. Fair is shown separately. Negative includes Poor and Very poor.
  • Unsure responses and uncategorizable demographic responses are excluded from relevant percentages.
  • Baytown uses supplied respondent-level survey data. The other 24 cities use deterministic synthetic data solely to demonstrate the dashboard design and interactions.
  • Percentages can vary slightly because of rounding and active filters.
